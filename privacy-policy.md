### Forma online rankings
When you choose to join Forma rankings, Firebase Authentication assigns an anonymous identity to that app installation. Eligible completed games store a generated player name, score and server update time in Cloud Firestore. You may optionally choose a public nickname (3–20 characters), stored with your anonymous app identity and server update time. Other participating players can see this nickname, or the generated name if you have not chosen one, alongside your scores. Please do not include private information in a public nickname. Your Google profile name and email address are not submitted to this leaderboard, and your Google Play Games profile may remain private.

This ranking identity is specific to the installation and may be lost when app data is cleared or the app is reinstalled. Clearing local data does not delete existing online records. Google sign-in does not automatically move this identity to another device. Existing Google scores are not automatically imported. To request removal of ranking data, contact maysoft.support@gmail.com and provide your displayed Forma player name; additional information may be needed to identify the correct record.

Forma also offers optional Google Play Games cloud backup and restore. These operate only when selected and are independent of Firebase ranking participation.

See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
